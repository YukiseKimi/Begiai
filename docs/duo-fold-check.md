# iPhone Duo: the fold check

The journeys run on the open Duo only. Check the fold and the half-fold by hand, on an iPhone
Duo or on the iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1 or later), before a release that changes the map,
the panel or the Line Diagram. In the simulator, change the pose from the Device Hub or from
the simulator's hinge control.

Start the app with the Duo open and flat, on the Map tab.

## Open, flat

1. The map fills the screen. The panel is a glass column on the right, about as wide as the
   iPhone's panel, from below the status bar to above the tab bar.
2. The Locate, About and compass buttons stand at the top right of the map, beside the column,
   not under it.
3. Tap a train. Its page opens in the column. Tap Follow: the map zooms to the train and keeps
   it in the middle of the map beside the column, not under the column.
4. Tap the map away from the trains: the page closes, and the column shows the lines.
5. Tap a station: its board opens in the column.
6. On the Line Diagram tab, tap a train: its page opens in a column on the right, and the
   diagram moves left, so no station is under the column. Close closes the column.

## Fold like a laptop (half-folded, hinge across the middle)

1. Open a train on the Map tab, then fold the Duo partly, like a small laptop.
2. The map is on the upper half and the panel on the lower half, the full width of the screen.
   Nothing sits on the fold: not the panel's edge, not a button, not a row.
3. The same train's page is still open, and Follow still follows it.
4. Open the Line Diagram tab with a station open: the diagram is on the upper half and the
   board on the lower half.

## Fold like a book (half-folded, hinge down the middle)

1. The map is on the left half and the panel on the right half.
2. The Locate button stands at the top right of the map's half, clear of the fold.

## Close, and open again

1. With a train open, close the Duo. The outer screen shows today's iPhone layout: the map
   with the panel as a sheet at the bottom, and the same train's page open in it.
2. Drag the sheet's handle: it steps between Small, Half and Full as on an iPhone.
3. Open the Duo again: the column comes back on the right, with the same train's page.
4. On the Line Diagram tab, do the same with a station open: closed, the board is in a sheet;
   open, it is in the column. The station stays open both ways.

Note anything that is wrong with the pose, the tab and what was open, and a screenshot.
