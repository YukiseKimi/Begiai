## Description
Bėgiai shows Lithuania's passenger trains moving on a map.

Open the app and see every LTG Link train that runs now, each on its real track, turned the way it goes. Tap a train for its stops and its next station. Tap a station for its next departures on a departure board. Star the lines and stations you use, and they come first.

The Line Diagram shows one line from end to end, with each train between two stations, as Japanese railway apps do. It also tells how often the trains run at rush hour and at midday.

HONEST ABOUT THE DATA
LTG Link publishes no live train positions. Bėgiai estimates each position from the official timetable, with a realistic speed model, and says so on every train. A late train still shows where it should be. The timetable updates itself each day, and the app tells you if its timetable gets old.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
VoiceOver, large text, Reduce Motion, larger train markers and a high-contrast mode. Line colours stay apart for colour-blind riders, in light and dark mode.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your stars stay on your iPhone.

Bėgiai is an unofficial app. It is not from LTG, LTG Link or LTG Infra. Timetable: UAB LTG Link, through the open GTFS of visimarsrutai.lt.

## Notes for the reviewer
Bėgiai is an unofficial timetable app with no account and no login. Train positions are estimates from the open GTFS timetable (UAB LTG Link, via visimarsrutai.lt); the app says so on every train page and in About. The app downloads a fresh timetable once a day from GitHub Pages. Tips are consumable and unlock nothing.
