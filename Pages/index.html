Bėgiai

Lithuanian trains on a map, estimated from the timetable.

What the app is

Bėgiai shows where the LTG Link passenger trains in Lithuania should be now, on a map and on a line diagram, with each station's next departures. LTG Link publishes no live positions, so every position is an estimate from the open timetable. A late train still shows where it should be.

Bėgiai is an unofficial app. It is not from LTG, LTG Link or LTG Infra.

Where the data comes from

  • Timetable: UAB LTG Link, through the open GTFS of visimarsrutai.lt. This site rebuilds it each night.
  • Track work and changes: LTG Link's notices.
  • Map: Apple Maps.

Help and feedback

Found a wrong train, a missing station or a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. During the beta, you can also send a screenshot from TestFlight.
