Privacy policy

Bėgiai · last changed 19 November 2026

Bėgiai collects no data about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

On your iPhone only

  • Stars and settings. The lines and stations that you star, and the display settings, stay on your iPhone.
  • Location. The app asks iOS for your location only after you tap Locate, to show you on the map. Your location stays on your iPhone and is never stored or sent.

What leaves your iPhone

  • The timetable. One time each day, the app downloads the newest timetable from this site, which GitHub Pages hosts. The download sends nothing about you; GitHub receives the request like any web request. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Tips. A tip is an in-app purchase that Apple handles. The app does not see your payment details.
  • Crash reports. If you allow it in iOS Settings, Apple shares anonymous crash data with the developer.
  • Links. The links to LTG Link and visimarsrutai.lt open their websites, which have their own policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
